Android packagein.azadinstitute.btsstudent
Effective date14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bilaspur Test Series, an Azad Institute venture based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, India, handles information in connection with the Bilaspur Test Series Student App. The app is primarily used by enrolled or otherwise authorized students to access academic tests, results, rankings, review features and support.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Android application identified by package name in.azadinstitute.btsstudent and to the student-service functions directly connected with that application.

Third-party websites, apps or services that you choose to open from outside the app are governed by their own privacy policies.

2. Information we process

Account and access information

To provide access to authorized students, we may process information such as:

  • your name;
  • your registered mobile number;
  • course, subject, batch or entitlement/access information; and
  • login or access credentials used to authenticate your eligibility to use the service.

Academic activity and performance information

When you use tests and result features, we may process academic activity including:

  • tests opened or attempted;
  • answers or responses submitted;
  • scores, attempt history and result-review information;
  • rank and percentile information; and
  • other progress information needed to provide performance and leaderboard features.

Where a leaderboard is available, it may display limited academic information such as a student's name, score, rank or percentile. We do not display a student's mobile number on the leaderboard.

Support and question-report information

If you voluntarily use Help, Doubt Support or Question Report features, we may process the information you provide for that request. A question report may include items such as question number, selected language and the issue or reason described by you.

If you choose to continue a support request through WhatsApp, the information you send through WhatsApp is also processed by WhatsApp/Meta under its own terms and privacy policy.

Technical and service information

Our systems and service providers may process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service, such as app version, request timestamps and basic network or server-request information.

3. Information the Play-distributed app does not collect for payment

The Google Play version of the Student App is an access/consumption app for students. The app does not collect or store payment-card numbers, UPI credentials, bank-account credentials or other banking authentication information.

4. Advertising

The Student App is not designed to display third-party advertising and does not use advertising functionality as part of its student-test experience.

5. How we use information

We use information only for purposes reasonably connected with providing and protecting the Student App and related student services, including to:

  • authenticate students and determine access eligibility;
  • deliver course and test content;
  • save attempts and provide results, review, rank, percentile and performance history;
  • respond to support, doubt and question-report requests;
  • maintain service reliability, security and abuse prevention;
  • diagnose technical problems; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations or valid legal requests.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be disclosed only where reasonably necessary to:

  • service providers: trusted hosting, database, infrastructure or technical-service providers that help us operate the app;
  • support services chosen by you: for example, when you choose to contact support through WhatsApp;
  • legal or safety purposes: where disclosure is required by law, legal process, or is reasonably necessary to protect users, our service or our rights; or
  • business continuity: in connection with a lawful reorganization, transfer or continuation of the service, subject to appropriate protection of user information.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or alteration. Data transmitted between the app and its online services is intended to use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections.

No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so users should also protect their login/access credentials and avoid sharing them with others.

8. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide student access, maintain academic records and result history, resolve support or security issues, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it, anonymize it, or securely retain only the portion that must be kept for legitimate legal, security or record-keeping purposes.

9. Access, correction and deletion requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your Student App access. To help us locate the correct record and prevent unauthorized changes, we may need to verify that the request relates to you.

For a deletion request, contact Bilaspur Test Series support and clearly state that you are requesting deletion of your Student App data. Where deletion is permitted and technically applicable, associated personal information will be deleted or de-identified, except information that must be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, security or legitimate record-keeping reasons.

10. Children's privacy

The Student App is intended for candidates and students preparing for teacher-recruitment, eligibility and competitive examinations. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age.

If we learn that information from a child has been provided in a manner inconsistent with applicable requirements, we will take reasonable steps to review and address it.

11. Third-party services

The app may rely on third-party infrastructure or may allow a user to voluntarily open an approved third-party service for a limited purpose such as support. Those providers may process information under their own privacy terms. Users should review the privacy terms of any third-party service they choose to use.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Student App, our data practices or applicable requirements change. The current version will be posted at this URL and the effective date above will be updated when appropriate.
