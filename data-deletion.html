Data Deletion Request

Bilaspur Test Series Student App · Android package: in.azadinstitute.btsstudent

Students can request deletion of personal data associated with their Bilaspur Test Series Student App access. Requests are handled manually after verification so that another person cannot delete a student's data without authorization.

How to request deletion

  1. Contact Bilaspur Test Series through the official WhatsApp support link below.
  2. Write: “Student App Data Deletion Request”.
  3. Provide the registered mobile number associated with your Student App access.
  4. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request relates to you.
  5. After verification, we will process the deletion request from our backend systems.
Request data deletion on WhatsApp

Data covered by a deletion request

Depending on the data associated with the student record, a deletion request may cover personal and app-related information such as:

  • student profile information linked to the registered mobile number;
  • Student App access/session information;
  • course or entitlement information linked to the student record;
  • test attempt and response history;
  • scores, results, rank/percentile and related academic activity.

Data that may need to be retained

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, fraud or abuse prevention, security, dispute resolution, or legitimate record-keeping. Where applicable, retained information will be limited to what is necessary for those purposes.

Important

The Google Play version of the app does not provide in-app account creation. Students enroll outside the Play app and receive access credentials for the Student App. This page provides a public method to request deletion of Student App data.
