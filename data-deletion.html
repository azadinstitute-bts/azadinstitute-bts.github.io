Account and Data Deletion

Bilaspur Test Series Student App · Android package: in.azadinstitute.btsstudent

You can request deletion of your Bilaspur Test Series Student App account and associated personal data, or request deletion of selected personal data while keeping your account. Requests are handled manually after ownership verification.

How to request deletion

  1. Email founder@bilaspurtestseries.com or use the official WhatsApp support link below.
  2. Write “Student App Account Deletion Request” to delete your account, or “Student App Data Deletion Request” and specify which personal data you want deleted while keeping your account.
  3. Provide the Google email or registered mobile number associated with your Student App access. Do not send passwords, OTPs, card details or UPI PINs.
  4. We may ask for limited information to verify that the request relates to you.
  5. After verification, we process the request and explain any data that must be retained. You can also open Help in the app, choose Delete account / personal data, and prepare your request.
Request account or data deletion on WhatsApp

Data covered by a deletion request

Depending on the data associated with the student record, a deletion request may cover personal and app-related information such as:

  • student profile information, including name, contact number and Google account identity;
  • Student App access/session information;
  • course or entitlement information linked to the student record;
  • test attempt and response history;
  • scores, results, rank/percentile and related academic activity.

Data that may need to be retained

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, fraud or abuse prevention, security, dispute resolution, or legitimate record-keeping. Retained purchase records may include order identifiers, purchase status and verification records. They are restricted to those purposes and kept only as long as necessary; we explain any applicable retention when handling your request.

Important

The app supports Google (Gmail) sign-in and existing Student App access credentials. Deleting your account removes its access and associated personal information, subject to the limited retention described above. Account deletion does not automatically refund a purchase. The 7-day payment-support request period does not limit account or personal-data deletion requests.
